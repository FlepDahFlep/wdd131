What is Psychosis?

Psychosis is a condition that affects the way a person interprets reality. It may involve hallucinations, delusions, or disorganized thinking, making everyday life difficult. Early recognition and treatment can greatly improve recovery.

Common Symptoms

  • Hallucinations
  • Delusions
  • Confused or disorganized thinking
  • Difficulty concentrating
  • Withdrawal from family and friends
  • Changes in emotions or behavior

Prevention is Better than Cure

Seeking professional help at the earliest signs of psychosis can improve long-term outcomes. Family support, understanding, and timely treatment play an important role in recovery.
