How Can You Help?

What You Should Do

  • Remain calm and patient.
  • Listen without judging.
  • Speak clearly and gently.
  • Encourage professional help.
  • Offer practical support.

What You Shouldn't Do

  • Don't argue about delusions.
  • Don't ridicule or shame them.
  • Don't force conversations.
  • Don't ignore warning signs.
  • Don't leave them alone if they're in immediate danger.

What to Do in Emergency Situations ❓

If someone is at immediate risk of harming themselves or others, stay with them if it is safe to do so, contact local emergency services, or take them to the nearest emergency department. Stay calm, speak in a reassuring voice, and avoid confrontation.
